RESEARCH PROJECT • MACHAKOS COUNTY, KENYA

Transforming Wholesale Shops Through Digital Solutions.

Exploring how digital technology can improve efficiency, simplify business operations, reduce errors and support the growth of wholesale businesses in Machakos County.

Explore Our Research

INTRODUCTION

About the Research

Wholesale shops play an important role in distributing goods to retailers and other customers. However, some businesses experience challenges associated with manual record-keeping, stock management, slow transactions and communication with suppliers. This research explores practical and affordable digital solutions that can help wholesale shops improve their operations, make informed decisions and serve customers more effectively.

OUR RESEARCH

Scope of the Study

The study focuses on selected wholesale shops in Machakos County, Kenya, and examines how digital technologies can improve business efficiency.

01

Geographical Scope

Selected wholesale shops operating within Machakos County, Kenya.

02

Content Scope

Inventory management, sales records, digital payments, customer service and supplier communication.

03

Target Population

Wholesale shop owners, managers, employees, suppliers and customers.

04

Time Scope

The study will be conducted over a defined research period to assess current practices and possible improvements.

THE PROBLEM

Challenges Facing Wholesale Shops

Depending on their current systems and resources, wholesale businesses may experience the following operational challenges.

Stock Management

Manual stock tracking can lead to inaccurate records, stock shortages and overstocking.

Record-Keeping

Paper-based records can be difficult to update, organize and retrieve when needed.

Slow Transactions

Manual calculations and payment reconciliation may delay customer service.

Communication

Delayed communication can affect ordering, restocking and customer relationships.

THE PROPOSED APPROACH

Digital Solutions

Affordable digital tools can help wholesalers organize their activities and manage information more effectively.

01

Inventory Management Systems

Track stock levels, record incoming and outgoing goods, and identify products that need restocking.

02

Digital Sales and Record-Keeping

Use computerized records to capture transactions, generate receipts and organize daily sales information.

03

Electronic Payments

Use mobile money, bank transfers and other suitable digital payment methods to simplify transactions.

04

Online Ordering and Communication

Use websites, messaging platforms and digital order forms to communicate with customers and suppliers.

05

Financial Reporting

Use spreadsheets or accounting software to monitor expenses, sales and estimated profits.

CONCLUSION

Building More Efficient Wholesale Businesses

Digital solutions offer opportunities for wholesale shops to improve stock control, record-keeping, financial management and customer service. The appropriate technologies will depend on each business's needs, budget, digital skills and access to reliable internet services. The study will examine these opportunities and identify practical recommendations for wholesale shops in Machakos County.
